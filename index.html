At a glance

This Privacy Policy explains how Nardi Royale handles information when you use the Android game. By using the game, you acknowledge the practices described below.

Information we process

Nardi Royale does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, or payment information. Game progress, virtual coins, settings, and statistics are stored locally on your device.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process device and app identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. This data may be used to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. Information needed to provide ads may be shared with Google AdMob and its advertising partners under their own privacy terms.

Data retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the game. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies. You can manage ad personalization in your Google account and Android privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, clear Nardi Royale's app data, or uninstall the game.

Children

Nardi Royale is not directed to children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so we can review the matter.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will always be available on this page.
